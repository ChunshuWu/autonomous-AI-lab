# WuLab Research

WuLab is organized into models. The **research model** defines the scientific mindset for two stages:

- **Proposal:** Choose a significant, narrow question with affordable computation and obtainable data.
- **Research:** Develop and test ways to use the proposed factor; find an option that works and check why, where, and at what cost.

Roles are researcher, engineer, mathematician, librarian, and orchestrator. All agents read the models for a shared understanding and perform their assigned duties:

- [research.md](research.md): scientific mindset and the two research stages.
- [communication.md](communication.md): independent preparation, peer meetings, voting, and engineer handoffs.
- [library.md](library.md): shared sources, reading packages, reusable methods, and lasting knowledge.
- [human-interface.md](human-interface.md): dashboard timelines, experiments, proposal and method maps, meetings, library browsing, and director feedback.
- [scheduling.md](scheduling.md): next actions, work permission, saved handoffs, sleep, wake-ups, and recovery.

Meetings have one discussion and one researcher vote, with engineer and mathematician advice. The human director chooses the next step; everyone receives that decision and the meeting conversations before work resumes. Research candidates are concrete experiment designs.

For every operational block, the orchestrator owns diagnosis, a permitted repair attempt and verification that the affected work continues. The service must supply the saved inputs and usable recovery actions; missing internal evidence is a handoff problem to repair, not a reason to ask the director to retrieve files WuLab already holds. Follow the recovery process in [scheduling.md](scheduling.md). Human decisions, holds and scientific stopping points remain binding.

Every role uses simple language in all writing, including titles, instructions, updates, questions, replies and documents. Explain unfamiliar terms before using them; follow the clear-writing guidance in the communication model.

Keep scientific reasoning in the research model and operating rules in the model responsible for them.
